\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textit{This geometric decomposition suggests practical uses beyond the
intervention analysis, especially for diagnosing compression error and
interpreting training-time allocation.} An auxiliary control observes the same
directional asymmetry at two internal MLP sites
(Appendix~\ref{app:mlp-internal-carrier}).

\paragraph{Compression diagnostics.}
Approximation error should not be viewed only as an overall residual magnitude.
Directional decomposition complements total error by showing whether
compression changes the update direction. In our results, perpendicular error
separates compression methods more clearly than parallel error. This suggests
measuring total approximation error together with its perpendicular component.

\paragraph{Training-time allocation.}
Inference-time edits probe models after training. We therefore ask whether
parallel suppression can instead be built into training from scratch. Keeping
the modified forward computation active during both optimization and evaluation
avoids a train--inference mismatch and lets the model adapt from initialization.
In our evaluated settings, both variants improve the downstream average, with
the largest gains from V-Para Rem. This shows that the role of a component
depends on the intervention space, including when the intervention is part of
training itself.
